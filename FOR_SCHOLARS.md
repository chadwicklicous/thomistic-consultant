# The Thomistic Consultant — A Research Tool for Aquinas

## What it is

The **Thomistic Consultant** is a research tool that lets you ask questions about the philosophy and theology of St. Thomas Aquinas and receive answers grounded in his **original Latin texts**, each with its exact scholarly citation.

It contains the complete *Corpus Thomisticum* — the critical edition of Aquinas' collected works — over 93,000 passages, searchable by meaning. Ask a question in plain English (or Latin), and it retrieves the actual passages that answer it, with references you can verify against the critical edition.

It is not a chatbot that paraphrases Aquinas from memory. It is a retrieval tool that grounds every answer in the primary source.

## How to use it (two steps)

The tool is designed for people who use **Hermes Agent** — an AI assistant. You do not need any technical knowledge.

1. **Install the skill.** In Hermes, run:
   ```
   hermes skills install https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chadwicklicous/thomistic-consultant/main/SKILL.md
   ```

2. **Ask it to set up.** Say to Hermes:
   > "Set up the Thomistic consultant."

Hermes will then install everything it needs, download the corpus, and build the search index — all on its own. This takes a few hours the first time, but you only do it once.

After that, simply ask questions like:

> "What does Aquinas mean by synderesis?"
> "How does Aquinas prove the existence of God?"
> "What is the relationship between faith and reason?"

And you will receive an answer quoting the original Latin, with the exact citation (for example, *Summa Theologiae* I, q. 2, a. 3).

## What it offers

- **The complete works of Aquinas**, in the original Latin, searchable by meaning
- **Exact citations** for every passage, so you can verify against the critical edition
- **Works not available elsewhere** — including texts restricted on the Bibliothèque nationale de France
- **No technical setup** — your AI assistant does all the work
