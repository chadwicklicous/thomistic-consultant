# The Thomistic Consultant — A Research Tool for Aquinas

## What it is

The **Thomistic Consultant** is a research tool that lets you ask questions about the philosophy and theology of St. Thomas Aquinas and receive answers grounded in his **original Latin texts**, each with its exact scholarly citation.

It contains the complete *Corpus Thomisticum* — the critical edition of Aquinas' collected works — over 93,000 passages, searchable by meaning. Ask a question in plain English (or Latin), and it retrieves the actual passages that answer it, with references you can verify against the critical edition.

It is not a chatbot that paraphrases Aquinas from memory. It is a retrieval tool that grounds every answer in the primary source.

## How to use it

The tool works through **Hermes** — an AI assistant that runs on your computer. You do not need to know anything about computers. You just talk to Hermes in plain English, the way you would talk to a colleague.

### Getting started (one time only)

The first time, the tool needs to be set up. This is a one-time thing, and it is easiest if a friend or colleague who is comfortable with computers does it for you — it takes about a minute. They will:

1. Open Hermes and paste in one line of text (the "install" line).
2. Tell Hermes: *"Set up the Thomistic consultant."*

Hermes then does everything else on its own — it installs what it needs, downloads the complete works of Aquinas, and builds the search index. This takes a few hours the first time, but it happens in the background and you only ever do it once.

### Asking questions (every day)

Once it is set up, you simply ask Hermes questions in plain English, like:

> "What does Aquinas mean by synderesis?"
> "How does Aquinas prove the existence of God?"
> "What is the relationship between faith and reason?"

And you will receive an answer that quotes the original Latin, with the exact citation (for example, *Summa Theologiae* I, q. 2, a. 3), so you can look it up yourself in the critical edition.

## What it offers

- **The complete works of Aquinas**, in the original Latin, searchable by meaning
- **Exact citations** for every passage, so you can verify against the critical edition
- **Works not available elsewhere** — including texts restricted on the Bibliothèque nationale de France
- **No technical knowledge needed** — you just talk to Hermes in plain English
